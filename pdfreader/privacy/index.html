01Who we are

PDF Reader (Android package com.hivecrawlstudio.pdfreader) is developed and published by HiveCrawl Studio (“we”, “us”). This policy covers the PDF Reader app and its optional Exact view download. It does not cover other apps or websites, including Google's, which have their own policies.

Where data protection law gives a role of “controller” or “data fiduciary”, that is HiveCrawl Studio for the limited information described here.

02What the app handles on your phone

To do its job, the app opens, creates and changes information that stays on your device. None of it is sent to us.

  • Your documents. PDFs, Office files, images and text files you open, and the files you create, merge, split, compress, convert, sign or edit.
  • Your library. A list of recent, starred and scanned files, reading positions, bookmarks, and changes you choose to keep in the app, stored in the app's private storage.
  • Search index. Text from your documents, kept on the phone so you can search inside files.
  • Scans and photos. Pictures you take with the camera to scan documents, and images you add to PDFs.
  • Recognised text. Text the app reads from scans and images (“Make searchable”), processed on the phone.
  • Signatures and your name. Signatures and initials you draw or type, and the name you set for comments and signing.
  • Voice comments. Audio you record with the microphone, saved inside the document you comment on.
  • Form suggestions. Things you type into PDF forms, offered again next time if you leave this setting on.
  • Settings. Your preferences, such as theme, reading mode and save behaviour.

You control this information. You can delete files, clear recents, turn off form suggestions, remove saved signatures, or erase everything by clearing the app's storage or uninstalling it.

03What we collect

We do not collect personal information. HiveCrawl Studio does not operate any server that the app sends data to. The app has no account system, no analytics, no advertising, no crash-reporting service of our own, and it does not use the Android advertising ID.

We do not sell, rent or trade personal information, and we do not use it for advertising or profiling.

If you email us, we receive your email address and whatever you choose to include, such as a description of a problem or a file you attach. We use it only to reply and help you, and delete it when it's no longer needed.

If you choose to share crash details through Android's or Google Play's feedback tools, or leave a review on Google Play, that information is handled by Google, and we may see it in the Google Play Console as Google allows.

06Purchases and subscriptions

PDF Reader is free to use, with some features available through a paid Pro subscription or purchase. All payments are processed by Google Play under the Google Play Terms of Service. We never see or store your card or bank details.

  • The app checks with Google Play whether you have an active Pro purchase, so it can unlock Pro features on your devices.
  • How many free uses of Pro tools you have left is counted on your phone only.
  • You can manage or cancel a subscription at any time in the Google Play app under Payments & subscriptions, or at play.google.com/store/account/subscriptions. Refunds follow Google Play's refund policies.

07Sharing and other apps

Your files leave the app only when you choose to send them. For example:

  • Share and Open with. When you share or open a file with another app, such as email, chat or cloud storage, that app receives the file and its own privacy policy applies.
  • Links. Tapping a web link inside a document opens it in your browser.
  • Read aloud. Uses your phone's text-to-speech engine. Depending on the engine you've chosen in Android settings, it may process text online.
  • Define. A word you choose to look up is passed to the dictionary app you pick.
  • Files in cloud folders. If you open or save files in a folder synced by a cloud service, that service handles them.

We do not share your information with anyone else, except where we are required to by law. Because we don't hold your documents or personal data, there is normally nothing for us to disclose.

08Backups

If you have turned on Android backup for your Google account, Android may include this app's settings and library data in your device backup, encrypted and stored by Google, so they can be restored on a new phone. Documents you saved to your phone's storage are not part of this app backup. You can turn backup off in Android settings under Google › Backup.

09Keeping and deleting data

  • Information on your phone stays until you delete it, clear the app's storage, or uninstall the app. Uninstalling removes the app's private data, including your library, search index, saved signatures and settings.
  • Files you saved to your phone's storage, such as PDFs you created or exported, stay where you saved them until you delete them.
  • We keep support emails only as long as needed to help you, and delete them on request.
  • Google keeps purchase and account data according to its own policies.

10Security

Because your documents are processed on your device, they're protected by your phone's own security, such as its screen lock and storage encryption. The app keeps its data in Android's private app storage, which other apps can't read. Files are shared with other apps only through Android's secure sharing, and only when you choose to share.

When you unlock a password-protected file, the password is kept only in memory while the app is open, and the unlocked copy is temporary. Passwords are never saved to storage or sent anywhere.

No method of storage is perfectly secure, so please keep your phone's lock and software up to date.

11Children

PDF Reader is a general-purpose productivity app and is not directed at children under 13, or under the age of digital consent where you live. We don't knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has sent us personal information by email, contact us and we'll delete it.

12Your rights

Depending on where you live, laws such as the EU and UK GDPR, India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act 2023, and California's CCPA/CPRA give you rights over your personal data. These include the right to access, correct, delete or move it, and to object to or restrict how it's used.

Because we don't collect personal data through the app, most of your information is already in your hands on your phone and you can view or delete it there. For anything you've sent us by email, contact us and we'll respond within 30 days. For data held by Google, such as purchases, use your Google Account settings or Google's own request process.

You also have the right to complain to your local data protection authority. We'd appreciate the chance to help first, so please get in touch.

We do not sell or share personal information for cross-context behavioural advertising, as those terms are defined under California law.

13International transfers

We don't transfer your documents anywhere. Google may process the limited information described in section 04 in countries other than yours, under the safeguards described in the Google Privacy Policy. If you email us, your message is stored with our email provider.
